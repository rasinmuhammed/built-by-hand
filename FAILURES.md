# What did not work

Dead ends, wrong assumptions, and things I had to rebuild. Appended as they happen, not cleaned up afterwards.

This file exists because a log containing only wins is evidence of nothing. It is also where the honest answer to *"tell me about a time you were wrong"* comes from, with a date and a cost attached, instead of improvised in the room.

**Format:**

```markdown
## MMM DD · One-line title

**Assumed:** what I believed going in
**Actually:** what was true
**Cost:** how much time it burned
**Lesson:** the transferable part
```
